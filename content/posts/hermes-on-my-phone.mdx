The chat is the least interesting part of a personal agent.

I wanted a stateful box I could share with Hermes Agent, where the filesystem holds the work and I'm not rebuilding context across a pile of threads. I can hand it parallel jobs, leave, and get a ping when it has a result or actually needs a decision.

Hermex puts that same session on my iPhone. I can dispatch something from my phone, continue from my desktop, or set up a recurring job in natural language without treating every interaction like a new session.

Tailscale handles access, with port 8787 nowhere near the public internet. The agent can keep its files, browser state, cookies, and secrets on one VPS, preferably Hetzner, with DigitalOcean as the fallback.

That state matters once you move past demos. Real work includes logins, inbound hooks, email, git, and sites that'll never ship the integration you want. The agent needs a computer, not another thin layer over chat.

Ramp Router sits in front of the models so I can see what the work actually costs and stay model-agnostic. One Responses API, model ids pulled from the catalog, no guessing vendor names from memory.
